# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing Next.js application with Clerk authentication, Prisma-backed data, Stream calls/chat, and supporting booking, payout, and email integrations.

## Users

Oralix serves interviewers and interviewees. Interviewers conduct interviews on the platform; interviewees participate in those interviews.

## Product Purpose

Oralix is an interview platform where interviewers can take interviews of interviewees and earn credits for conducting them. Interviewers can convert earned credits into money.

## Positioning

Oralix connects interview participation with an interviewer earning mechanism: interviewers are rewarded with credits for completing interviews, with those credits convertible to money.

## Operating Context

The product supports interviewer and interviewee workflows including authentication, onboarding, interviewer discovery, interviewer profiles, availability and booking, appointments, interviews/calls, chat, credits, earnings, payouts, and related email notifications.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve all currently working business logic, integrations, data contracts, authentication behavior, booking behavior, interview/call behavior, credit accounting, and payout behavior unless explicitly approved otherwise.
- The visual and experience layer is intentionally open for revision across the entire website.
- Future UI work may change typography, colors, design language, UI components, spacing, logo, animation, motion, responsive presentation, and UX flows while preserving the underlying working logic.
- User-provided design references will define the future visual direction.

## Brand Commitments

The product name Oralix is confirmed. Further logo, typography, color, voice, and visual identity decisions remain open and will be supplied through future design direction.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains the existing runnable web application and its current interface. No external design reference has been provided yet. Future work must not invent testimonials, customers, benchmarks, pricing, or other unsupported product claims.

## Product Principles

- Preserve reliable interview and earning workflows.
- Keep interviewer and interviewee responsibilities clear.
- Make interview participation and interviewer rewards understandable.
- Treat the existing UI as replaceable while protecting working product behavior.
